Why Bees Matter

Bees and other pollinators are responsible for 1 in every 3 bites of food, and pollinate 80% of plants. Despite being vital to human survival, they still face danger due to a lack of bee-friendly spaces.

🌎

Bees Support Entire Ecosystems

By pollinating a vast array of plants, bees are a keystone species.

🍎

Bees' Role in Food Systems

1 in 3 bites of food is directly attributed to pollinators.

Initiatives

📚

Education

At Bee A Savior, we believe that education is the first step toward protecting pollinators. Through presentations, interactive activities, videos, and community events, we teach students and families about the vital role bees play in our ecosystems and food supply.

🌻

Pollinator Gardens

Pollinator gardens provide essential habitat and food sources for bees and other pollinators. Bee A Savior works to establish and support gardens that promote biodiversity while serving as outdoor learning spaces for communities.

🐝

Fundraising

Our fundraising events turn awareness into action. Through the sale of handmade beeswax candles, merchandise, and community-driven campaigns, we raise funds to support educational programs, pollinator gardens, and outreach efforts.
