Why Bees Matter

Bees and other pollinators are responsible for 1 in every 3 bites of food, and pollinate 80% of plants. Despite being vital to human survival, they still face danger due to a lack of bee-friendly spaces.

Bees' Role in Food Systems

🍎

Essential Crops

Bees are crucial for producing popular crops, such as apples, almonds, and cucumbers.

🌼

Pollination

Furthermore, these small insects are also responsible for pollinating 80% of all flowering plants worldwide.

🌎

Global Food Systems

There is a critical relationship between bees and global crops, with pollinators contributing an economic value of over $200 billion.

Without bees, global food systems would be far less stable, and likely collapse.

Bees Support Entire Ecosystems

By pollinating a vast array of plants, bees are a keystone species.

🌱

Plant Growth

Plants pollinated by bees grow seeds, fruits, and nuts: and are crucial producers in food chains.

🌿

Healthy Habitats

These flowers don't just provide essential food for herbivores: their roots prevent soil from eroding, allowing for safe habitats.

🌸

Stronger Plants

Bees also allow for cross-pollination across diverse plant species, creating stronger, genetically varied plants that are more resistant to disease.

If bees were to disappear, scientists estimate more than 100,000 species of plants could go extinct.

Threats

🏗️

Habitat Loss

Bees and other pollinators face danger due to a lack of bee-friendly spaces.

🧪

Pesticide Exposure

Many commercial pesticides interfere with pollinator navigation, behavior, and overall survival in general, even at low levels.

🌎

Environmental Challenges

Bee A Savior focuses on increasing awareness about bees and other pollinators, and the environmental challenges they face, including habitat loss and pesticide exposure.

What Can We Do?

🌻

Plant Community Gardens

Community gardens create small but meaningful habitats where bees can feed, pollinate, and rest. These spaces, filled with bee-friendly flowers, help restore biodiversity in urban areas, where natural ecosystems have been replaced by buildings, roads, and lawns.

🌱

Use Safer Pesticides

Using homemade pesticides or other alternatives reduces bees' exposure to harmful chemicals that are usually contained in conventional pest deterrents. Healthier pest management practices help create safer environments for pollinators while still maintaining gardens and green spaces.

📣

Spread the Word

Letting others know about this key issue is essential for increasing public understanding of pollinator decline and encouraging action. Through conversations, educational events, social media, and school-based initiatives, accurate information can reach and motivate large audiences, scaling into broader community change.
