About Bee A Savior

Bee A Savior is a student-led movement educating others and building real-world solutions to protect pollinators.

How We Started

Bee A Savior began in elementary school, when I first heard that bees could face extinction within a few years. Concerned, I made some hand-drawn posters and placed them around my neighborhood mailbox to spread awareness. Even though they were eventually taken down, that moment sparked a lasting interest in pollinators and their role in the environment.

That early curiosity slowly grew into action. I began creating and selling bee-themed candles to support awareness efforts, while also speaking to others about the importance of pollinators.

Over time, I expanded this mission through social media, using short educational videos to reach a wider audience.

Today, Bee A Savior continues to focus on one goal: helping others understand the importance of bees and inspiring action through education, creativity, and community engagement.

🌼 Our Mission

Bee A Savior exists to educate communities about pollinators and support environmental awareness through education, conservation, and student leadership.

🐝 Our Impact

We've educated more than 600 community members, raised $1000 for bees, and informed 100,000 people virtually about the importance of bees.

What We Do

📚

Education

At Bee A Savior, we believe that education is the first step toward protecting pollinators. Through presentations, interactive activities, videos, and community events, we teach students and families about the vital role bees play in our ecosystems and food supply.

🌱

Pollinator Gardens

Pollinator gardens provide essential habitat and food sources for bees and other pollinators. Bee A Savior works to establish and support gardens that promote biodiversity while serving as outdoor learning spaces for communities.

💛

Fundraising

Our fundraising events turn awareness into action. Through the sale of handmade beeswax candles, merchandise, and community-driven campaigns, we raise funds to support educational programs, pollinator gardens, and outreach efforts.
