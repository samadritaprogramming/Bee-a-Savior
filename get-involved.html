Bring Bee A Savior to Your School

Start a student-led chapter focused on bee education and community action. Chapters organize events, spread awareness, and support local environmental initiatives.

As a chapter founder, you will receive sample curriculums, event plans, and extra resources to help you succeed. You will also be able to earn certified volunteer hours, develop leadership positions, and be eligible for awards!

📚

Teach communities why bees matter and how to protect them.

🌱
